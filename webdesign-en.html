// How we work

Wherever you are.

The whole project runs remotely. You don't need to visit our office: a phone call, a few emails and a preview link are enough.

  1. 01

    Intro call

    A phone or video call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to do.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    Price, pages and delivery time, in writing, before we start.

  3. 03

    Design and preview

    You watch the site take shape on a private link, on your phone or laptop, and tell us what to change.

  4. 04

    Launch

    It goes live on your own domain and we submit it to Google and Bing so it starts showing up in search.

// FAQ

What people usually ask before we start.

How much does a website cost?

From €500 + VAT. The final price depends on the number of pages, the languages and any special features, such as bookings or 3D models. You get a fixed written quote before we start.

Do you work with clients outside Greece?

Yes. Everything runs remotely, by phone, video call and email. We work with clients across Greece, Cyprus and the rest of the European Union, in Greek or English.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size. A one-page site takes less time than a bilingual site with many service pages. The delivery date is written into the quote.

Will my site show up on Google and ChatGPT?

We build it so search engines and AI tools can read it properly: fast loading, clear titles, structured data and text that is visible without JavaScript. After launch we submit it to Google and Bing. Nobody can honestly promise the first position, but you start from a solid base.

Why no WordPress?

Because a business website doesn't need it. Without WordPress there are no plugins to keep updating, the site loads faster and there is far less to attack.

Can the site be bilingual?

Yes, and most of the sites we've built are, in Greek and English. Each language is written to read naturally, not like a machine translation.
